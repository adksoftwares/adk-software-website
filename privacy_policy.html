Privacy Policy for Cafe POS

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

App: Cafe POS (com.adksoftwares.nilacafe)

Developer: ADK Software Solutions

Key Summary: Cafe POS is an on-premise, secure Point of Sale and Billing application designed specifically for restaurant operations. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your business or personal data. We do not display third-party advertisements or use ad trackers.

1. Information We Collect

Cafe POS collects and processes only the information necessary to fulfill billing, inventory, and restaurant operations:

  • Order & Transaction Records: Purchased items, quantities, pricing, order channel (Dining, Takeaway, Table), and timestamps.
  • Restaurant Information: Store name, contact number, and address displayed on generated invoices and receipts.
  • Customer Information (Optional): Name and phone number optionally entered by the cashier for customer loyalty records or order reference.

2. Data Storage & Security

All sales records, product catalogs, and shift logs are stored securely on the local device and/or the store's designated private backend server. We implement role-based access control and local encryption standards to protect store operational data.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE: Used solely to synchronize sales and orders with the store's local server and network printer endpoints.
  • USB / PRINTER (Optional): Used to communicate with connected ESC/POS thermal receipt printers and cash drawers.
  • DOWNLOADS / STORAGE: Used exclusively to save and export generated PDF invoices and sales reports upon user request.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, transmit, or disclose any store data, sales metrics, or customer information to any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Store administrators retain full control over their sales history and product catalog. You can edit, delete, or clear local database records directly within the application settings.
